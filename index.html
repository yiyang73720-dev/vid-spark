FAQ

Common questions

What kind of videos do you create? +
Short-form vertical videos (15–60 seconds) optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Product showcases, behind-the-scenes, before/after transformations, tips, and trending formats.
Do I need to film the raw footage? +
We can work with quick phone clips you film (we send a simple shot list), or we use premium stock footage and AI-enhanced visuals. No fancy equipment needed.
How fast do I get my videos? +
Standard delivery is 5 business days. Pro plan clients get priority 3-day delivery. First batch may take an extra day as we learn your brand.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel, pause, or change your plan at any time. You stay because you want to, not because you have to.
What if I don't like the videos? +
Every plan includes revisions. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, we'll refund your money — no questions asked.
Can you post the videos for me? +
Yes! On our Pro plan, we handle posting on an optimized schedule. Starter and Growth plans get ready-to-post videos with captions — just upload.
Who makes my videos? +
Professional video editors, copywriters, and social media strategists. Every member is vetted and trained on the latest short-form trends and platform algorithms. We accept less than 1% of applicants.
