Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of videos do you create?

We create short-form vertical videos (15-60 seconds) optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. This includes product showcases, behind-the-scenes content, customer testimonials, before/after transformations, educational tips, and trending format videos.

Do I need to film the raw footage myself?

It depends! We can work with footage you film on your phone (we'll send you a simple shot list), or we can use premium stock footage and AI-enhanced visuals. Most clients send us quick phone clips — no fancy equipment needed.

How fast do I get my videos?

Standard delivery is 5 business days from when we receive your footage. Pro plan clients get priority 3-day delivery. First batch may take an extra day or two as we learn your brand style.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, absolutely. No contracts, no commitments. You can cancel, pause, or change your plan at any time. We believe our work speaks for itself — you stay because you want to, not because you have to.

What if I don't like the videos?

Every plan includes revisions. If you're not happy with a video, we'll rework it until you are. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, we'll refund your money — no questions asked.

Can you post the videos for me?

Yes! On our Pro plan, we can handle posting to your social media accounts on an optimized schedule. For Starter and Growth plans, we deliver the final videos and captions ready-to-post — just upload and go.

Who are the people making my videos?

Our creative team consists of professional video editors, copywriters, and social media strategists. Every team member is vetted and trained on the latest short-form video trends and platform algorithms. We accept less than 1% of applicants.
